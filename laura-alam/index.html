Optional extra: the brochure

Most of our clients don't have one. The ones who do wonder how they managed without it.

I'm including this because it comes up with almost everyone we work with, and it's rarely something they've thought of themselves. The problem it solves is simple: after a call, a parent goes away to think, to talk to their partner, to compare you with the charity and the solicitor. Right now they take nothing with them except what they can remember. Which means the decision gets made without you in the room. That matters, especially when the subject matter is so emotive and a considered purchase that could change lives, literally.

A brochure fixes that. Your story and why you do this. The EHCP process explained the way you explained it to me. The three ways of working, and what each one includes. The team, with their photos and their backgrounds. Your reviews. Built to make a parent want to work with you, and to make an associate feel like a safe pair of hands.

It's digital, so you send it after every call and the team sends it too. It goes on the landing page as a download and in the email sequence. And it can be turned into a printed version very easily for events like Together for SEND.

Again, a mockup, not the finished thing. Four of the pages, put together from the call and your website, so you can see the shape of it.
