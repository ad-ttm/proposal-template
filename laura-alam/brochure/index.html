Why I do this

I'm not going to tell you it's easy. I'm going to tell you what to do next.

I've got six children. One of them is non-speaking and needs a very high level of support. I've been through the EHCP process as a parent, and I've felt what you're feeling at one in the morning with a refusal letter open on the kitchen table.

I'm also a registered nurse, trained to Advanced level in SEND law, and for the last seven years I've led cases for families across England. Most of my families come to me after they've been told no.

The EHCP process is a legal one. It has deadlines, sections and rules the local authority has to follow. Most parents don't know that, and the system is counting on it. We do know it. That's the difference.

Three ways to work with us. You choose how much you hand over.
