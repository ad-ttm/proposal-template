Why I do this

I'm not going to tell you it's easy. I'm going to tell you what to do next.

I've got six children. One of them is non-speaking and needs a very high level of support. Two have autism and ADHD. I've been through the EHCP process as a parent, and I've felt what you're feeling at one in the morning with a refusal letter open on the kitchen table.

I'm also a registered nurse, trained to Advanced level in SEND law, and for the last seven years I've led cases for families across England. Most of my families come to me after they've been told no.

Know your deadlines

The clock is already running.

The EHCP process is statutory. That means the local authority has legal deadlines, and so do you. Miss yours and you lose the right to challenge. Three of the ones that matter most:

20weeks, the maximum legal time to issue an EHCP
15days to comment on a draft plan
2months to appeal to the SEND Tribunal after a final decision
